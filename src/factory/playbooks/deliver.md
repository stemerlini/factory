# Deliver

Goal: the outputs someone else can use, in `out/`.

1. Produce the deliverables (BOM, Gerbers, STEP, drawings, release, report) with
   studies of gate `deliver`, so they are reproducible.
2. Check them: completeness against the requirements, versions, a quick sanity check.
3. Summarise what was delivered in the proposal; the user approves and locks.
