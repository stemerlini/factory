# Concept

Goal: one chosen way of working, with the options considered and why this one.

1. Write `concept/concept.md`: principle of operation, two or three options, a comparison
   against the driving requirements, the choice and why.
2. Back the comparison with numbers (rough budgets are fine); a quick script study is
   better than prose.
3. If the choice is not obvious, `factory ask` and let the user decide.
