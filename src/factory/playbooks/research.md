# Research

Goal: the design stands on known physics, prior art and standards, cited.

1. Search the user's library first (Citadel: `citadel search`), then the literature.
2. Add the citekeys that matter to `references:` in `element.md`; add papers worth keeping
   to Citadel.
3. Write a short research note per topic in the vault folder for this element: what is
   known, numbers that bound the design, open questions. Cite, don't paraphrase vaguely.
4. Flag anything that changes a requirement as a proposal or a decision; do not change
   requirements silently.
